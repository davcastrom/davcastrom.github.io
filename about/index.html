About me

I am a Chilean plant scientist and postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Engineering Sciences, Universidad de O’Higgins. My work focuses on the interactions among plants, fungi, and soil, and on how these systems respond to environmental change.

Since 2026, I have been studying the recovery of soil fungal communities after forest fires at UOH. Previously, I worked on tree–fungal ecology and litter–soil interactions at the University of Copenhagen, and on forest fertilization and ectomycorrhizal communities at the Umeå Plant Science Centre, SLU.

I earned a PhD in Biology from the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences, an MSc in Biological Sciences (Arid-Zone Ecology) from the University of La Serena, and a degree in Biology Education. My research toolkit includes field and laboratory experiments, fungal community profiling, metagenomics, RNA-seq, multi-omics, and statistical analysis in R and Python.

I also enjoy video games and learning about computation and programming.

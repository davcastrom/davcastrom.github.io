<section class="section" id="research">
  <div class="section-heading"><div><p class="section-label">Research areas</p><h2>Forest ecosystems in focus</h2></div></div>
  <p class="section-intro">My research follows the connections between plants, fungi, and soil, from molecular responses to the recovery of forest communities after disturbance.</p>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="research-card"><span class="card-index">01 / CURRENT PROJECT</span><h3>Forest-fire recovery</h3><p>At the Universidad de O’Higgins, I study the recovery of soil fungal communities after forest fires and how these communities change through time.</p></article>
    <article class="research-card"><span class="card-index">02 / FOREST ECOLOGY</span><h3>Tree litter &amp; fungal traits</h3><p>How tree species, litter quality, and soil properties shape ectomycorrhizal fungal traits and community composition.</p></article>
    <article class="research-card"><span class="card-index">03 / PLANT–MICROBIOME</span><h3>Plant–soil feedbacks</h3><p>How plants interact with soil microorganisms, nitrogen availability, and fungal communities during establishment and growth.</p></article>
    <article class="research-card"><span class="card-index">04 / MOLECULAR ECOLOGY</span><h3>Stress &amp; multi-omics</h3><p>Combining transcriptomics, metagenomics, and physiological measurements to study plant and microbial responses to environmental stress.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

      <p class="section-label">Profile</p><h2>Research across systems</h2>
      <p>I am a Chilean plant scientist and postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Engineering Sciences, Universidad de O’Higgins. Before returning to Chile in 2026, I worked at the University of Copenhagen and the Umeå Plant Science Centre at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences.</p>
      <p>I hold a PhD in Biology from SLU, a Master’s degree in Biological Sciences with a specialization in arid-zone ecology, and training in biology education. Across these roles, I have worked on forest ecology, fungal communities, plant stress, and plant–microbiome interactions.</p>
